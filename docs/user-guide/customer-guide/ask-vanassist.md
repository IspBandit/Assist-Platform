# Ask VanAssist

## Purpose
Ask VanAssist is the primary plain-language way to find relevant caravan and RV providers, stays and reviewed traveller facilities.

## Intended users
Australian caravan, camper and motorhome travellers, including visitors who are not signed in.

## Permissions
No account is required. Ask is available only on VanAssist when the production feature flag is enabled.

## Fields
Enter what you need and a town, postcode or “near me”. “Use my location” shares the current location for that search after browser permission.

## Actions
Submit the question, review the interpreted place and radius, switch between list and map, open a result, use category search, or request assistance.

## Workflows
1. Describe the problem or destination need in everyday language.
2. Include a location or safely use device location.
3. Check the interpreted town, distance and result labels.
4. Confirm capability, availability and directions directly with the provider or source.

For fire, gas, failed brakes, medical emergencies or immediate danger, move to safety and call Triple Zero (000). Ask is not emergency dispatch or diagnosis.

## Examples
- `Public toilets and dump points near Batehaven NSW`
- `My caravan fridge is warm in Bundaberg`
- `Free camp within 50 km of Longreach QLD`

## Common mistakes
- Omitting the state for a duplicated town name.
- Treating a related provider as confirmation that it handles the exact fault.
- Relying on straight-line distance as road distance.
- Entering personal contact or medical details in the question.

## Related pages
Use `/find` for category and town controls, `/stays` for the full stays search, and `/request-assistance` when no suitable listing is found.

## FAQ
**Does Ask invent businesses?** No. Deterministic interpretation routes to platform records and reviewed facilities.

**Is paid AI required?** No. The initial production experience uses deterministic interpretation with paid AI disabled.
