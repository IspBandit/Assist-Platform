# TrailerWise services and marketplace

## Purpose
TrailerWise primarily helps users find trailer repairers, manufacturers, dealers, parts suppliers, inspectors and compliance specialists. Sale and hire listings are secondary.

## Intended users
Trailer owners, buyers and hirers seeking Australian trailer services or listings.

## Permissions
Public discovery requires no account. Providers need an owned provider account to create or edit a trailer listing.

## Fields
Service discovery supports category and location. Marketplace filters include keywords, trailer type and sale, used or hire availability.

## Actions
Open provider profiles, confirm services and contact details, filter marketplace listings and review seller-supplied listing information.

## Workflows
1. Start with the service directory for repairs, inspection or compliance help.
2. Use marketplace filters only when looking for sale or hire inventory.
3. Verify identity, ownership, condition, registration, ratings and payment arrangements independently.

## Examples
Search trailer services by town before opening a provider profile. A marketplace listing marked pending is not publicly available until reviewed.

## Common mistakes
- Assuming TrailerWise has inspected or guaranteed a listing.
- Paying before independently checking the trailer and seller.
- Using a marketplace listing instead of qualified inspection or compliance advice.

## Related pages

## FAQ
**Is TrailerWise mainly classifieds?** No. Specialist and business discovery is the primary experience.

**Does editing keep a listing live automatically?** Provider edits return the listing to pending review.
