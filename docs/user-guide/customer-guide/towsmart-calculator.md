# TowSmart calculator

## Purpose
TowSmart helps users understand a loaded towing combination against entered manufacturer limits. It is guidance, not certification or legal advice.

## Intended users
Australian drivers comparing a tow vehicle with a caravan, camper or trailer.

## Permissions
Calculation, guide and checklist access are public. Saving a combination requires the signed-in owner.

## Fields
Choose catalogue records or enter vehicle and trailer ratings manually, then add passengers, fuel, cargo, water and accessories.

## Actions
Calculate the combination, review each margin, consult source guidance, use the checklist and save a result snapshot.

## Workflows
1. Verify every rating against the exact vehicle and trailer plates or current manufacturer material.
2. Enter expected real loading.
3. Review all individual limits, not only the overall status.
4. Recalculate after load changes and obtain professional weighing or engineering advice where needed.

## Examples
Use manual entry when the exact model year and grade are absent. A green result means the entered figures are within the known inputs; it is not approval to tow.

## Common mistakes
- Selecting a similar but different catalogue grade.
- Entering tare values as loaded values.
- Omitting towball load, passengers, accessories, fuel or water.
- Treating the result as legal certification.

## FAQ
**Does TowSmart replace a weighbridge?** No.

**Can I edit or compare saved combinations?** Saved snapshots are available; richer edit, delete, compare and reporting remain planned.
