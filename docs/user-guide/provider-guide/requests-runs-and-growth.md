# Requests, runs and growth

## Purpose

Work with requests assigned to the provider and use analytics, service-run or growth capabilities when those modules are enabled for the active brand.

## Intended users

Authenticated users authorised for a provider record.

## Permissions

The provider role gate and provider ownership checks apply. Module availability is brand-dependent; a route existing in the repository does not mean every brand enables it.

## Fields

Request responses and outcomes use the status and response fields shown for an assigned match. Service runs use title, timing, towns, services and supported status fields. Growth credentials and campaigns use the evidence, targeting and budget fields presented by their forms.

## Actions

Open assigned requests, respond, record an outcome, view provider analytics, manage enabled service runs, submit capability evidence, withdraw evidence, and create or update supported growth campaigns.

## Workflows

Review an incoming request before responding. For a run, save the main record before adding towns or services. Growth credentials and campaigns enter review workflows; submission alone does not make a capability or campaign public.

## Examples

A provider can respond to an assigned request, then later record the supported outcome. A submitted capability credential remains pending until administrative review.

## Common mistakes

- Assuming every provider can see every request.
- Claiming a credential is approved immediately after submission.
- Assuming a growth campaign can override organic relevance.

## Related pages

## FAQ

**Why is a module absent?** The active brand may not enable it, or the provider account may lack an eligible record.

**Are campaign placements guaranteed?** No. Campaign activation and delivery remain subject to review, targeting and budget checks.
