# Privacy policy — Assist Platform brands

**Effective 18 September 2026.** Published for platform use; formal solicitor review may refine wording later. Not legal advice.  
**Operator / APP contact entity:** Glen Condren (sole trader), ABN 76 553 821 887 (“we”, “us”, “our”).  
**Services covered:** VanAssist, TowSmart and TrailerWise websites and related Platform services.  
**Contact for privacy:** prefer the brand support address; otherwise support@vanassist.com.au.

This policy explains how we collect, hold, use and disclose personal information. We aim to handle personal information consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles and to comply with the *Privacy Act 1988* (Cth) where it applies to us. We also consider the *Spam Act 2003* (Cth) for electronic marketing.

---

## 1. Scope

1.1 This policy applies to personal information about individuals who visit or use VanAssist, TowSmart or TrailerWise, create accounts, submit requests, claim provider listings, contact us, or otherwise deal with us online.

1.2 It does not cover the privacy practices of independent providers, parks, payment processors or other third-party websites you choose to use.

1.3 LocalTorque and Polaris are not active public brands and are outside the current public product scope.

---

## 2. What we collect

Depending on how you use the Platform, we may collect:

| Category | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Identity and contact | Name, email, phone, town/postcode, business contact details |
| Account | Login identifiers, password hash, roles/permissions, MFA enrolment data if enabled |
| Profile and preferences | Saved listings, saved towing combinations, notification preferences |
| Service / assistance content | Request descriptions, private address details you supply, vehicle/registration details you supply, messages, uploaded images |
| Provider / park business data | Business name, ABN, services, service areas, licences/insurance evidence when requested, claim evidence, consent records |
| Location | Optional device coordinates or derived town/region; we may round or limit precision for longer-term analytics where implemented |
| Technical / security | IP address, browser/device information, security and rate-limit logs, session identifiers |
| Analytics | First-party page-view and usage events (route, referral, session/user identifiers as configured) |
| Consent and audit | Consent type/version, grant/revoke time, IP/user agent where recorded; administrative audit logs |
| Transactional | Email delivery records; billing/subscription records if paid features are enabled |
| AI / search (where enabled) | Search prompts, limited response metadata, usage/budget events, knowledge-gap signals subject to retention jobs |

We do **not** require you to upload verification documents unless requested for a claim, verification or compliance process. Restrict sensitive documents to what is requested.

---

## 3. How we collect it

- Directly from you (forms, accounts, uploads, email, phone).
- From people authorised by you.
- From providers or parks in connection with a request or listing you interact with.
- Automatically through website use (sessions, security logs, first-party analytics when enabled).
- From lawful public or licensed sources for clearly marked unclaimed business listings.
- From subprocessors who help us host, send email, resolve maps/routes, or (if enabled) provide AI, CAPTCHA or payments.

If you provide information about another person, you must be authorised to do so and should tell them about this policy where reasonable.

---

## 4. Why we use personal information

We use personal information to:

- create and operate accounts and authenticate users;
- display directories, stays, facilities and related discovery tools;
- process assistance requests and connect users with providers;
- support service-run and provider workflow features where enabled;
- operate TowSmart calculators and saved combinations;
- verify or review provider claims and evidence;
- respond to enquiries and complaints;
- prevent fraud, abuse and security incidents;
- measure demand, improve coverage and product quality;
- send service/transactional messages;
- send marketing only where permitted, with unsubscribe options;
- meet legal, accounting and dispute obligations;
- transfer the business subject to appropriate safeguards if we sell or restructure.

We do **not** sell personal information.

---

## 5. Location, matching and automated processing

5.1 Location access is optional where offered. Coordinates may be used to find nearby towns, stays, facilities or providers and to prepare directions.

5.2 Search ranking and matching may use automated rules (category, distance, service area, availability, verification). These assist discovery and do not make legal, credit, employment or insurance decisions.

5.3 Where AI-assisted search is enabled, prompts and outputs may be processed by an approved model provider under our instructions and configuration. Do not submit unnecessary sensitive information into free-text prompts.

5.4 Provider verification and material account actions may involve human review.

---

## 6. Who receives information

We disclose personal information only as reasonably needed to:

- providers or parks you contact or ask us to match;
- authorised staff and contractors bound to confidentiality;
- hosting, CDN/DNS, email, security, analytics, maps/routes, and (if enabled) AI, CAPTCHA or payment providers;
- professional advisers;
- regulators, courts or law enforcement where required or appropriate;
- a purchaser or successor if the business is transferred, subject to appropriate safeguards.

Public provider profiles may contain business information. Unclaimed listings are identified as such and may be corrected, claimed or removed after verification. Personal service requests and private customer contact details are **not** published as open directory listings.

## 8. Cookies and similar technologies

8.1 Essential cookies or storage support security, sessions and preferences.

8.2 When first-party analytics are enabled, we may use a randomly generated session identifier to count visits and understand page, search and provider-interest activity. Analytics reporting is designed not to store visitor IP addresses for anonymous reporting; security logs may separately record technical information to protect the service.

8.3 We do not rely on a third-party advertising pixel suite by default. Optional advertising technologies will be used only as disclosed and subject to applicable consent choices.

8.4 External maps, linked sites and payment pages have their own practices.

---

## 9. Security

We use access controls, encryption where appropriate, logging, backups and operational safeguards. No online system is risk-free. You should use a strong unique password and enable MFA when offered for administrative or high-risk accounts.

---

## 10. Retention

We retain personal information only while needed for the purposes described, legitimate business records, dispute handling, security and legal obligations, then delete or de-identify it where reasonably practicable.

**Engineering defaults observed in product documentation (require legal approval before treating as the public commitment):**
- AI search/usage style events: often configured around 180 days;
- AI gap events: often configured around 365 days;
- analytics retention jobs exist in code/operations;
- soft-deleted user records may remain until a verified erasure process is completed;
- backups can retain copies until backup rotation removes them — deletion must be considered across live systems and backups.

We will publish or confirm approved retention schedules as they are finalised. Until then, requests are handled case-by-case consistently with law and security needs.

---

## 11. Access, correction, deletion and portability-style requests

You may request:
- access to personal information we hold about you;
- correction of inaccurate information;
- deletion or de-identification where practicable;
- information about consents you have given.

We may need to verify your identity. We may refuse or limit a request where permitted by law (for example legal holds, fraud prevention, security logs, or records we must keep).

To exercise these rights, email the brand support address with “Privacy request” in the subject line.

---

## 12. Direct marketing

We send marketing only where permitted (for example consent or another lawful basis). You can unsubscribe using the link in a marketing message or by contacting us. Transactional service messages may still be sent when needed to operate your account or a request.

Providers who receive customer details through the Platform must not use them for unsolicited marketing contrary to the Spam Act or Privacy Act.

---

## 13. Children

The Platform is not directed to children under 18. A parent or guardian should contact us if they believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate authority.

---

## 14. Data breaches

We assess suspected eligible data breaches. Where the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme applies, we will notify affected individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) as required.

---

## 15. Complaints

Contact us first. We will acknowledge and investigate within a reasonable time. If you are not satisfied, you may contact the OAIC: https://www.oaic.gov.au

We may update this policy as the service or law changes. Material changes will be identified on this page and, where appropriate, notified directly to registered users.
