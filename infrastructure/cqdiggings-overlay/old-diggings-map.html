How to use it

Follow evidence, not a promising-looking dot.

  1. Filter to the evidence relevant to your method.
  2. Turn on uncertainty halos before comparing old workings with modern terrain.
  3. Open the permanent record and linked reports.
  4. Use modern geology and drainage to form a testable source-to-trap hypothesis.
  5. Check exact-ground access separately before any field visit.
Research-next areas are deliberately broad. They identify documentary and sampling questions. They are not secret spots, discovery predictions or permission to fossick.
