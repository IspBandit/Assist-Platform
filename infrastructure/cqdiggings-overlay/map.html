Historical evidence

Experimental research layers are off by default. They preserve research history but are not validated prospectivity.

Density layers are optional. Turn one on to see where official records cluster; individual markers stay readable with them off.

The main green layer follows official watercourses downstream from records that explicitly mention a reef, lode, vein or quartz source. It becomes stronger where evidence is stronger and is marked “supported” when nearby alluvial records also match the corridor.

What the potential colours mean
Weaker model supportStronger model support

Deep green means stronger modelled likelihood, not a guaranteed find. It does not show how much gold remains, where a deposit ends, whether the land is public or whether you may enter.

The separate known-record layer simply shows where official records cluster. Turn it on to compare raw evidence with modelled pathways.
